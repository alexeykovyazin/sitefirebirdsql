EmberWings

The quarterly magazine about Firebird

EmberWings is the official publication of the Firebird Project. IBPhoenix launched it in March 2024. After three issues, the Firebird Foundation took over the publication.

Each issue has technical articles, guides and tips, interviews with noteworthy people, Firebird development updates, community news, product reviews and a touch of thematic humor.

  • Free for everyone, 3 months after release
  • Early access for Firebird Foundation donors
  • Read online or download the print-friendly PDF
