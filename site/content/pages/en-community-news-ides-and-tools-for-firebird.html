IDEs and tools for Firebird

Check out new versions and new tools for Firebird:

Plamenix 1.0.0-beta

Open-source IDE for Windows, Linux, and macOS. It works as a web application and as a standalone executable.

Download from Github, and share your feedback/bugs found.

Why check it out: a rare self-hosted web application that also supports Windows, Linux, and macOS. It also has a permissive MIT license, so it's genuinely free and open.

Beekeeper Studio

Beekeeper Studio is a modern and easy to use database manager that supports over a dozen databases and has full feature parity on MacOS, Linux, and Windows. Commercial product with a free version.

Download here

Why check it out: an interesting IDE with multi-database support — worth trying.

RdbExpert 2026.8

A new version of the RdbExpert tool is available. Written in Java, works on Windows and Linux.

Download here

Why check it out: a solid IDE with a lot of features, with a Java-style look and feel.
