Firebird Seminars in Siegburg (Germany) and Prague (Czech Republic)

Firebird seminars took place in Siegburg (November 22, 2013) and Prague (November 25, 2013). 39 and 50 attendees were there, photo reports are available at Facebook page of FirebirdSQL. Stay tuned for the next seminar date and place! The speakers were Vlad Khorsun (Firebird Core Team), Alexey Kovyazin (IBSurgeon), and Dmitry Kuzmenko (IBSurgeon).

More details about seminar schedule: http://www.firebirdsql.org/en/firebird-conference-tour-2013/
