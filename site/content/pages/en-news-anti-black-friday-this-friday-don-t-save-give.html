Anti-Black Friday: this Friday, don't save. Give.

Every day, your work runs on open source software. Firebird keeps your data safe. Dozens of libraries live in your code. You never got an invoice for any of it. Behind that code are people who fix bugs at night and on weekends. Many of their projects run on less money than one office spends on coffee.

Most of the time, they do not even ask.

Black Friday is the day to pay less. Anti-Black Friday is the day to pay for something you already use for free. Support the open source projects that you use, and start with the one that holds your data. For Firebird, that is €10 a month for a person, or €35 a month for a company that runs it in production. The Firebird Foundation turns this money into grants for the developers. Then forward this email to one colleague who also uses open source.

The discounts end on Monday. Your support does not.

  • Support Firebird as a person - €10/month
  • Support Firebird as a company - €35 a month

P.S. If you already support Firebird: thank you! Your part today is the easy one: please share link to this announcement.
