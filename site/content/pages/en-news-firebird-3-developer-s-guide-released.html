Firebird 3 Developer's Guide released

The Documentation team is proud to announce that the Firebird 3 Developer's Guide, by Denis Simonov, is now available in English.
This volume consists of chapters that walk through the development of a simple application for several language platforms, notably Delphi, Microsoft Entity Framework and MVC.NET (“Model-View-Controller”) for web applications, PHP and Java with the Spring framework.
Translating this guide from Russian and preparing it for publication in our library were made possible by a crowd funding campaign last year.  Warm thanks to all of you who contributed.
