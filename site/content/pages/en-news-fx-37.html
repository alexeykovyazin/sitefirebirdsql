November 20, 2012

ADO.NET Provider 3.0.2 Sub-release

As it turned out, there was a small regression in 3.0.1, so Jiri Cincura had to release ADO.NET Provider 3.0.2. You can download it in the ".NET Provider" section of Downloads.

ADO.NET Provider 3.0.1 Sub-release

Jiri Cincura announces the 3.0.1 sub-release of ADO.NET provider for Firebird. You can download it in the ".NET Provider" section of Downloads. Enjoy!

Firebird 2.5.2 sub-release is available

Firebird Project is happy to announce general availability of Firebird 2.5.2 — a minor release of the award-winning relational database management system.

ADO.NET Provider 3.0.0 Release and DDEX Provider 3.0.0 Release

Today Jiri Cincura announced the 3.0.0 release of ADO.NET provider for Firebird and the 3.0.0 release of the Firebird DDEX Provider for Visual Studio 2005/2008/2010/2012. You can download them in the ".NET Provider" section of Downloads. Enjoy!

News & Events
October 12, 2012

DRB Systems goes from Gold to Platinum sponsorhip

DRB Systems (http://www.drbsystems.com/), producer of POS systems for carwash industry (see case study) decided to upgrade their sponsorship from Gold to Platinum this year. Firebird Project developers and Firebird Foundation Committee would like to thank DRB Systems and personally Steve Summers for the ongoing support of Firebird SQL. Monetary support of Firebird development is especially important due to the fact that Firebird 3 is going to be released next year.

News & Events
September 17, 2012

Jaybird (Firebird JDBC driver) Survey

The Jaybird team would like to invite developers and users of Jaybird to fill out a survey on your use of Jaybird (Firebird JDBC driver). You can find the survey at http://infopoll.net/live/surveys/s36021.htm. The results of the survey will be used to decide where to focus development of the upcoming Jaybird versions.

Firebird 2.5.2 Release Candidate Available

The Firebird Project team is happy to announce that the release candidate for the Firebird 2.5.2 sub-release is ready for testing. This includes packages for Linux, Windows and Mac OS X platforms, as well as the source code tarball.
