Firebird Recovery Service (Vendor: IBSurgeon)

IBSurgeon provides professional database recovery services for heavily corrupted Firebird databases. When the FirstAID automated recovery tool cannot show or recover data from your corrupted database, or when you encounter problems with nbackup, or when dealing with very large databases, IBSurgeon's manual recovery service becomes the essential solution.

Before Purchasing Recovery Service

  1. Don't panic. Carefully check your server, your test server/computer, your backup locations, your colleagues's computers for the old good copies of the production database.
  2. Make a copy of your database. It is necessary to have an original copy of database for multiple iterations of recovery. Keep copy on another computer.
  3. Try to open your corrupted database with FirstAID tool, run check and preview data (it does not require license). FirstAID can be cheaper and faster alternative than manual recovery service.
  4. Try to compress database with zip, 7z, or rar. The idea of this step is to verify that database file was not filled with zeroes and still consists information. If compressed database will be become like 1000 times less than the original file, the database is filled with zeroes, and cannot be recovered.
  5. Consider price of the service and cost of manual recovery, may be it will be cheaper to re-insert data.

When You Need Recovery Service

Professional recovery services are required when:

  • IBSurgeon FirstAID tool cannot display or recover data from your corrupted database.
  • You experience problems with nbackup operations.
  • Your database is very large.
  • Multiple tables are corrupted simultaneously.
  • System areas of the database are damaged.
  • Record-level corruption requires manual intervention.

Remote Desktop Recovery Access for The Service
