Merry Christmas and Happy New Year 2021! And short review of 2020

Firebird Project wishes you a wonderful and prosperous holiday. Merry Christmas and Happy New Year 2021! 
Take a look at the list of the most important releases, events, articles, news in 2020!
Firebird Releases
  • Firebird 4.0 Beta 2, May 2020
  • Firebird 3.0.7 October 2020 (earlier in 2020 were 3.0.5 and 3.0.6, both are substituted with 3.0.7)
  • Firebird 4.0 RC1 - tag for source code of Release Candidate 1 was put December 31, builds and release notes to be released in January!
Support groups now are at Google Groups
May 2020 – due to the closing of Yahoo Groups, support groups were moved to Google Groups:  
  • firebird-support  
  • All groups are listed here
Events
  • In September 2020 in Brazil took place Firebird Developers Day #17, in the online format (in Portuguese)
Active Telegram channels
  • FirebirdSQL (English)  
  • Meu querido Firebird SQL (Portuguese)  
Documentation, articles, materials
Documentation
  • During 2020, Firebird Documentation was changed from DocBook to Asciidoc, a more convenient and flexible format. Many documents were reviewed and updated: www.firebirdsql.org/en/documentation/
