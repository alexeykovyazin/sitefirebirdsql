Announcing the Official Firebird Certification Program

The Firebird Foundation is proud to announce the Firebird certification program, designed to enhance the skills of Firebird developers and DBAs, and to assist employers in identifying skilled professionals.

Program Structure

  • Two levels: Basic and Advanced
  • Two exams per level: Developer and DBA

For detailed exam descriptions, please visit: https://firebirdsql.org/certification

Demo Exam Available Now!

Test your readiness with our demo exams, mirroring the structure of the actual certification tests.

Currently available: Firebird Basic DBA demo exam

  • 17 randomly selected questions from our question bank
  • 80% score required for successful completion

We encourage you to try the demo exam and assess your Firebird knowledge!

Benefits and Conditions

  • Basic level certified specialists receive a public profile on the FirebirdSQL certification website.
  • Advanced level certified specialists may be introduced to top Firebird-using companies (contact the Firebird Foundation President for details).
  • Additional benefits to be announced soon.

Stay Informed

For the latest updates and more information about the certification process, subscribe to the Firebird Certificate Substack.
Don't miss this opportunity to validate your Firebird expertise. Start your certification journey today!
