Firebird 6 session at ITDevCon in Italy

Fabio Codebue will be speaking at ITDevCon, Italy, on May 23, 2025.
Session Description:
Firebird 6 speaks JSON… and it's more structured than ever! In this session, we'll explore two of the most innovative features coming to Firebird 6: native JSON support and advanced schema management.
We'll examine how schemas improve data isolation, multi-tenancy, and security, making Firebird even more suitable for modular and distributed architectures.
Then we'll dive into the core highlight: JSON support. Firebird 6 will be able to store, query, and manipulate JSON documents directly within the database—natively and efficiently—without relying on external tools or workarounds.
Through real-world examples, live SQL code, and practical use cases, we'll demonstrate how to leverage Firebird 6 in modern scenarios like REST APIs, dynamic frontends, microservices, and data integration.
