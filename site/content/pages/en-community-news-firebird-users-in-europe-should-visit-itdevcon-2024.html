Firebird Users in Europe Should Visit ITDevCon 2024!

ITDevCon 2024 is the leading European conference on Delphi and related technologies, scheduled for November 14th and 15th, 2024 at the bit Time Group headquarters in Rome. This event, organized by bit Time Professionals and bit Time Software, will be the 13th edition of ITDevCon. It is a must-attend conference for Delphi developers, with more than 20 sessions, workshops, and networking opportunities for professionals, enthusiasts, and students. The language of the conference is English.

Wide Range of Topics:

The conference will cover the latest features in Delphi, integrations with Python, IoT solutions, RESTful architectures, mobile and web application development, and emerging trends like machine learning and AI integration. It also includes hands-on discussions on UI/UX best practices, design patterns, TDD, and Domain-Driven Design (DDD)

Networking Opportunities:

ITDevCon is a great place for networking with other professionals, industry experts, and decision-makers in the Delphi community. The event fosters an environment conducive to sharing ideas and building collaborations, which is crucial for the growth of any developer or IT professional BITTIME

Special Sessions for Firebird Users

As a Firebird user, you'll find value in sessions on integrating Delphi with databases, using RESTful APIs, interfacing with external services, and developing scalable web and mobile applications. This knowledge is directly applicable to leveraging Firebird's capabilities for various business applications.

Conference site: https://www.itdevcon.it/roma/
