Practical examples of migration to Firebird 5: databases 1.5Tb and 810Gb

In this video, we explore two case studies of successful migrations involving large Firebird databases, one at 1.5TB and the other at 810GB.

We delve into the server hardware specifications, crucial migration parameters, as well as the duration of backup and restoration processes, among other details.

Please download the presentation used in this video from here.
