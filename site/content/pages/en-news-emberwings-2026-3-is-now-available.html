EmberWings 2026/3 Is Now Available

We're pleased to announce that the September 2026 issue of EmberWings (2026/3) is now available.

This edition considers what happens when familiarity becomes a shortcut. In database work, the names may be known, the tools may be trusted, and the first answer may sound convincing. The important differences often appear later, when a system changes, a workload grows, or an old assumption meets a new environment.

The September issue follows that boundary between recognition and understanding, with the usual mix of technical perspective, project life, and the wider Firebird community.

As always, EmberWings is presented in a clean, print-friendly format designed for comfortable reading, whether you prefer reading on screen or on paper.

The latest issue is available exclusively to Firebird Associates and Firebird Partners. It will be available to the general public in December 2026.

Also, the June 2026 issue is now available to all readers.
