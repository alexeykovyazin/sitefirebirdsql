16th Firebird Developers Day (FDD), Aug 3, in Piracicaba, Brazil

The 16th Firebird Developers Day (FDD) will take place in Piracicaba-SP, Brazil, in August, 3.
Registrations are open at www.FirebirdDevelopersDay.com.br.

FDD is the biggest Firebird Conference in the world (in number of attendees). All Portuguese speakers are invited to visit the new conference site, check the topics and register!

Several tools and components will be sold with very special discounts during the conference day, offered by our sponsors and supporters like IBSurgeon, FastReports, IBObjects, IBExpert, ACBr, uniGUI, TeeChart, Woll2Woll and Gnostice.

Among the Brazilians specialists are Carlos Cantu, author of three Firebird books, including the Migration Guide to Firebird 3, and Alexey Kovyazin (IBSurgeon) as a special international speaker.

Here is a list of the (already) confirmed sessions:
 
  • What to do when your customer's database gets bigger (Carlos Cantu)
  • Developing nTier systems accessing Firebird  (Marcelo Daibert)
  • Developing OO software with Firebird  (Marcelo Daibert)
  • Understanding transactions control and deadlocks  (Alexandre B. Smith)
  • FB 4 - Changes in garbage collection and isolations (Alexandre B. Smith)
  • HQBird 2019 - Even more powerful! (Alexey Kovyazin)
  • How to optimize slow queries (Alexey Kovyazin)
  • Creating your first dashboard in the clould (Wagner Ramos)


More sessions will be added soon.

Friday 14, June is the last day to register for the conference before the price grows. Hurry up, save money and guarantee your seat at the conference!
