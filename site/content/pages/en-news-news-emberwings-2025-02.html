EmberWings 2025/2 (June) is Here!

The latest issue of EmberWings – our official quarterly Firebird magazine – is here!

This 76-page issue brings together the best of what our community has to offer: two in-depth featured articles, an insightful interview with a core Firebird developer, and an overview of the project’s ongoing development. All of this is complemented by updates from Firebird community, candid evaluations of noteworthy tools, practical takeaways, and—true to EmberWings style—a touch of thematic humor. All of it is presented in a clean, print-friendly layout designed for enjoyable reading, whether on screen or on paper.

The latest isssue is available only to all Firebird Associates and Firebird Partners. It will be available to the general public in September 2025. Also, the March 2025 issue is now available to all readers.
