The Firebird Conference 2016 will take place on October 7-8, 2016, in Prague, Czech Republic, at the Barcelo Praha Hotel.

There will be 2 simultaneous threads for the two full days. 

Main speakers

  1. Dmitry Yemanov, Lead architect of Firebird Core Team
  2. Vlad Horsun, Lead developer of Firebird Core team
  3. Alex Peshkoff, Lead developer of Firebird Core Team
  4. Jiri Cincura, Firebird .NET driver developer
  5. Roman Simakov, RedSoft and Firebird Core Team
  6. Philippe Makowski, IBPhoenix, Firebird Linux packages maintainer, Vice-president of Firebird Foundation
  7. Dmitry Kuzmenko, IBSurgeon
  8. Paul Reeves, IBPhoenix,
  9. Pavel Cizar, IBPhoenix
  10. Jason Wharton, IBObjects
  11. Wiesław Poszewiecki, CloudaIDE
  12. Alexey Kovyazin, IBSurgeon

Accommodation

Despite the fact that the Firebird Conference will be at the Barcelo Praha Hotel, we are not asking you to stay there. Check the local neighbourhood, as there are other accommodation options. There are also other hotels within walking distance that may provide you with cheaper accommodation:

  • Oaza
  • Panorama
  • Vladar

To get to the Barcelo Praha Hotel, take the metro Red "C" line to the Budejovická station from the Central Train Station. More detailed travel information can be found on the Hotel's website.
