64-bit V.2.1 RC1 Builds for MacOSX Intel

Firebird 2.1 RC1 builds for MacOSX 10.5 x86 are ready to download and test.

February 09, 2008

Rebuilt Firebird 2.0.3 MacOSX Superserver Kits

New kits for the Superserver installations on both i386 and PPC can be downloaded now. No changes to the engine but an installer problem has been fixed.

January 30, 2008

Rebuilt Firebird 2.0.3 Ports for MacOSX on PowerPC

The ports of Firebird 2.0.3 Superserver and Classic for MacOSX PPC have been rebuilt with some fixes. New builds for Classic and Superserver PPC are available via the Firebird 2.0.3 Downloads page.

Windows Snapshots Resume

Infrastructure problems are fixed and the Windows snapshots are again available.

Roadmap 2008

News & Events
January 23, 2008

Firebird 2.1 Release Candidate Ready

The Firebird Project team is happy to announce that download kits for the first V.2.1 release candidate are now available — Windows and Linux 32-bit and 64-bit platforms now, MacOSX soon. You are invited to test it furiously and report your experiences (good or bad) back to the Firebird-devel list. As always, please be patient if the files you want haven't made it out to the mirrors at your first attempt.

News & Events
January 17, 2008

Rebuilt Firebird 2.0.3 Ports for MacOSX on Intel

The ports of Firebird 2.0.3 Superserver and Classic for MacOSX have been rebuilt with a fix for a Services Manager issue. New builds for Classic and Superserver x86 are available via the Firebird 2.0.3 Downloads page. Rebuilds for PowerPC will appear soon.

News & Events
January 11, 2008

Jaybird 2.1.2 is Released

Jaybird 2.1.2 is released. It is a bugfix release that targets two main issues, JDK 6.0 compatibility and support for INSERT...RETURNING statements. More info at the JDBC Driver page.

News & Events
January 07, 2008

Firebird 2.0.3 64-bit Port for HP-UX 11

Paul Beach and Alex Peshkov release the port of Firebird 2.0.3 64-bit Classic for HP-UX 11 (PA-RISC 2.0). New link added to the Firebird 2.0.3 Downloads page.

News & Events
December 19, 2007

Firebird 2.0.3 Ports for MacOSX

Paul Beach and Alex Peshkov release the ports of Firebird 2.0.3 Superserver and Classic for MacOSX x86 and PowerPC. New link added to the Firebird 2.0.3 Downloads page. Phew! Porting don't get furiouser than this! All this activity has been made possible by the recent generous donation of a spanking new Macbook to the Firebird Project by an anonymous donor.
