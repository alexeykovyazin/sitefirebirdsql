Configuration Calculator for Firebird was Updated To Support Firebird 5

The Configuration Calculator for Firebird enhances Firebird setup by crafting configurations that surpass the default settings.

While the default firebird.conf is intentionally modest to ensure compatibility with less powerfull servers, leveraging high-performance hardware requires a more sophisticated configuration. This free tool generates configurations tailored to your hardware or virtual machine's specifications.

The updated release now supports Firebird 5 and offers an essential, yet practical analysis of the database header.
