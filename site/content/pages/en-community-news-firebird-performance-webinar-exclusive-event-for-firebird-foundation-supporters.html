Firebird Performance Webinar: Exclusive Event for Firebird Foundation Supporters

The Firebird Foundation is excited to announce its first exclusive webinar, this time on Firebird Performance!

  • Date: November 26, 2024
  • Time: 14:00 Central European Time (CET)
  • Length: 2 hours
  • Platform: Zoom

This in-depth webinar will explore performance and scalability aspects of Firebird, with a focus on optimizing the interplay between your Operating System, hardware, and Firebird configuration. Our goal is to help you extract maximum performance from your existing setup.

The webinar will be split into two parts:

  • Presentation: Gain expert insights on how to fine-tune Firebird for peak efficiency.
  • Live Q&A: Ask your specific questions directly to our expert panel.

The session will be conducted in English, and a recording will be made available for download to all participants.

Speakers:

  • Vlad Khorsun (Firebird Core Developer)
  • Alexey Kovyazin (Former President of the Firebird Foundation, CEO of IBSurgeon)

Who Can Attend?

This exclusive event is open to Firebird Foundation supporters, based on your membership level:

  • Firebird Associates — Eligible for 1 ticket (become Associate now)
  • Firebird Partners — Eligible for up to 4 tickets (become Partner now)
  • Sponsors — Eligible for 5+ tickets (please contact us for details)

If you’re a member of Firebird Foundation Australia with an active membership valid through the webinar date, you’ll also receive an invitation.

How to Join?

We will send a Zoom link to all eligible attendees via the email registered with your Firebird Foundation account prior to the event.

Not yet a supporter or sponsor? Become a Firebird Supporter today and unlock access to this exclusive webinar, along with many other rewards. Learn more about our programs https://store.firebirdsql.org/.

Don’t miss this opportunity to deepen your Firebird knowledge and enhance your database performance! If you have any questions, feel free to contact us via email.
