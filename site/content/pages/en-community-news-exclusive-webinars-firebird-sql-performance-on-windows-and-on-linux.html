Exclusive Webinars: Firebird SQL Performance on Windows and on Linux

Two-Part Webinar Series: Firebird Performance on Windows and on Linux

We're excited to announce our upcoming webinar series focused on optimizing Firebird SQL Database performance across different platforms. Join our database experts for practical insights and techniques to maximize your Firebird performance.

Webinar 1: Firebird on Windows: Performance, Tuning & Monitoring

Date: March 25, 2025, 14:00 CET

Speakers: Vlad Khorsun, Firebird Project core developer and Alexey Kovyazin, IBSurgeon

Discover essential strategies for monitoring your Firebird database in Windows environments. Our experts will demonstrate tools and techniques to identify bottlenecks and optimize performance for your Windows-based Firebird instances.

Webinar 2: Firebird on Linux: Performance, Tuning & Monitoring

Date: April 8, 2025, 14:00 CET

Speakers: Alex Peshkoff, Firebird Project core developer and Alexey Kovyazin, CEO IBSurgeon

Explore performance optimization techniques specifically tailored for Linux deployments. Learn platform-specific monitoring approaches and tuning methods to ensure your Firebird database runs at peak efficiency on Linux systems. 

Who should attend: 

Database administrators, developers, and IT professionals working with Firebird SQL Database who want to enhance performance and reliability.

Language: All webinars will be conducted in English.

Eligibility: These exclusive webinars are available only to Firebird Supporters and Sponsors as a benefit of your continued contribution to the Firebird Foundation. How to Register:

  • Current Firebird Supporters will receive a Zoom link via email one day before each webinar.
  • Not yet a Supporter? We recommend becoming a Firebird Supporter now to secure your participation in these valuable sessions.

Q&A Session: Each webinar will conclude with a dedicated question and answer session, giving you the opportunity to address your specific Firebird performance challenges with our experts.
