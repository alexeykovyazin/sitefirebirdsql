The last chance to register at Firebird Conference 2016

October 5, 2016, the registration to Firebird Conference 2016 will be closed! Hurry up, there is still a chance to visit Firebird Conference 2016, communicate with Firebird developers and leading Firebird experts. 

The conference will be devoted to the performance optimization of Firebird, high availability, and security. Don't miss it!
