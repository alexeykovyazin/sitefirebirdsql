25-April-2026 - Firebird VIP Seminar in Sao Paulo, Brazil (in Portuguese)

On April 25th, Alexey Kovyazin, CEO of IBSurgeon and also Vice President of the Firebird Foundation, and Edson Gregorio, CEO of MQFS, will be hosting the Firebird VIP Seminar (em Portuguese) in São Paulo/SP!

The Firebird VIP Seminar was previously held in 2022 in cities such as Piracicaba, Florianópolis, and Blumenau, with the goal of identifying the specific problems of each participating company and solving them!

On 04/25/2026, we have both IN-PERSON and ONLINE tickets! If you want to see real problem-solving and practical methods to improve performance and use AI, now is the time!

At the IN-PERSON event, you'll have the advantage of having us look closely at your database, apply REAL optimizations to your queries, and get answers to your specific questions.

More details at https://mqfs.com.br/fbvipsp2026

Agenda:

  • SQL Optimizations: Common Mistakes and AI
    • Multiple indexes instead of a composite index
    • Wrong field order in the composite index
    • ORDER index vs. SORT — when each is better
    • And much more!
    • AI without hallucinations: tools and methods in practice
  • Database Structure Analysis and Refactoring – what can we do beyond changing the page size?
    • Structure: larger tables and wide records — how to optimize
    • Indexes: key width, depth, selectivity, and cardinality
    • Advanced analysis tools and methods
  • Discussion of Your Databases and Queries In-person attendees may submit their databases, statistics, and queries (trace) for analysis
  • New HQbird Features: Materialized Views, Super Monitoring, AI Optimizations
    • Materialized Views for BI and more
    • SuperMonitoring — a new feature based on the Firebird core
    • IBSurgeon AI tools
  • Moving to the Cloud: Practical Steps, Typical Mistakes, Cloud Selection
    • Migration checklist
    • Cloud selection: speed tests
    • Critical and common mistakes
    • Practical testing of your databases on a real cloud server (send us your database in advance so we can upload it to the server and configure access)
