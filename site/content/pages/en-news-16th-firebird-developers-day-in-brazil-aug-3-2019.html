16th Firebird Developers Day in Brazil: Aug 3, 2019

The 16th Firebird Developers Day (FDD) will take place in Piracicaba-SP, Brazil, in August, 3.
Registrations are open at www.FirebirdDevelopersDay.com.br.

FDD is the biggest Firebird Conference in the world (in number of attendees). All Portuguese speakers are invited to visit the new conference site, check the topics and register!

Several tools, components, etc. will be sold with very special discounts during the conference day.

Among the Brazilians specialists is Carlos Cantu, author of three Firebird books, including the Migration Guide to Firebird 3, as well as Alexey Kovyazin (IBSurgeon) as a special international speaker.
