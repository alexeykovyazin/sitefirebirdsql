Announcing the 22nd Firebird Developers Day

Announcing the 22nd Firebird Developers Day (Online) - The Premier Portuguese-Language Firebird Conference

The FDD team is pleased to announce the 22nd Firebird Developers Day (FDD), which will be held online from October 6-10, 2025.

This event is the most important conference for Portuguese-speaking Firebird users, bringing together top specialists, core developers, and community members to share in-depth knowledge, practical experiences, and the latest news about the Firebird database.

This is a unique opportunity for the Brazilian and Portuguese-speaking community to learn from leading experts in their native language.

Featured Talks and Speakers:

Get ready for a week of high-level technical content. The confirmed sessions include:

  • Taking Firebird server to the Cloud by Carlos H. Cantu.

  • mcpFirebird: The Bridge Between AI and Your Firebird Data by Jhonny Suárez.

  • Reforma Tributária - from Theory to Technical Implementation by Elisabete Jussara Bach.

  • Practical Monitoring and Performance with MON$ by José Francisco Cervi Neto.

  • What to Expect from Firebird 6 by core developer Dmitry Yemanov.

Registration Information

Registration is now open, with a special early-bird price available.

  • Early-Bird Price (until September 5): R$ 150 (BRL)

  • Regular Price (after September 5): R$ 200 (BRL)

Special Discount: Arcade game fans can get a 10% discount by scoring over 1,000 points in the AsteroidesDB game!

Don't miss this chance to connect with the Firebird community and deepen your database expertise.

For the full schedule, speaker details, and to register, please visit the official website www.firebirddevelopersday.com.br
