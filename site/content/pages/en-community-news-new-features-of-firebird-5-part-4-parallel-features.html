New Features of Firebird 5, Part 4: Parallel backup, restore, sweep, and index creation

Part 4 of "Detailed New Features of Firebird" covers the parallel features of Firebird 5: how gbak improved the speed of backup and restore, and how gfix and index creation became faster as well. You will also find practical recommendations and results of tests.

All published parts to the moment are listed below:

  1. Detailed New Features of Firebird 5. Part 1: Improvements in Optimizer
  2. Detailed New Features of Firebird 5. Part 2: SQLs
  3. Detailed New Features of Firebird 5, Part 3: SKIP LOCK
  4. Detailed New Features of Firebird 5, Part 4: Parallel Features: backup/restore/sweep, creation of indices

There will be more 2 parts and the final bonus document, so stay tuned!
