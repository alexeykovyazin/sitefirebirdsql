About the Firebird Foundation

The Firebird Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing the development of the Firebird relational database as an open-source project. Our mission is rooted in fostering innovation, collaboration, and sustainability within the Firebird ecosystem.

As outlined in our Articles of Association, the Foundation’s purpose includes:

  • Supporting the Development of Firebird: We actively support the ongoing development of the Firebird database and related management systems to ensure its future as a powerful, open-source solution.

  • Managing Resources for Growth: The Foundation is responsible for raising and managing funds through dues, subscriptions, donations, and sponsorships. These resources are disbursed as grants to fund the development of Firebird and its surrounding infrastructure.

  • Fostering Collaboration: We encourage partnerships with individuals, non-profit organizations, and commercial companies who are involved in or plan to contribute to the development, support, and promotion of Firebird and its related projects.

  • Building a Cooperative Community: We promote cooperation with all individuals and organizations that share our vision, working together to ensure Firebird’s continued success as a global, open-source project.

Through these efforts, the Firebird Foundation plays a key role in maintaining a vibrant, sustainable community around Firebird, ensuring that it remains a leading choice for relational database solutions worldwide.

Join us in shaping the future of Firebird!

The Firebird Foundation offers multiple ways to support the future of Firebird while enjoying exclusive benefits. All allow you to be part of a dedicated community that actively contributes to Firebird’s ongoing development, but each offers a different level of engagement.

Sponsorship

Stable funding is crucial for ensuring the ongoing development and success of Firebird as a leading open-source relational database. Your financial contributions directly support the evolution of Firebird, from maintaining its core infrastructure to driving new innovations. While all donations are valuable, regular donations provide the foundation for sustained growth, allowing us to plan and execute long-term development goals more effectively.

We recognize and reward this stable support with exclusive benefits that increase based on your level of contribution. To make it easier for individuals and small businesses to support Firebird, we offer convenient subscription programs, available on a monthly or annual basis and payable by credit card. These programs are tailored to meet different levels of involvement and impact:

  • Firebird Associate: Our entry-level program designed for individuals who wish to contribute regularly and stay connected to Firebird's progress. As an Associate, you’ll enjoy early access to EmberWings Magazine, special offers from our partners, certification exam discounts, access to closed webinars, discounted conference fees, and occasional special gifts.

  • Firebird Partner: The advanced level of support, ideal for organizations or individuals seeking deeper engagement with the Firebird community. In addition to all the benefits of the Associate level, Partners gain access to exclusive technical content, the opportunity to participate in Firebird release planning, and more significant perks, including enhanced special offers and exclusive invitations.

For sponsors who choose to contribute beyond the Partner level, we offer a completely extraordinary level of cooperation. These larger donations are typically sent directly to our bank accounts and come with all the benefits of the Firebird Partner program, plus additional perks such as priority access, as well as tailored recognition for your contribution. This sponsorship arrangement is based on mutual agreement, allowing us to customize benefits to meet the specific needs and goals of our high-level sponsors

One-Off Donations:

For those who prefer to make a single contribution, we offer two convenient options, both of which come with our heartfelt thanks and memorable gifts as a token of appreciation for your support:

  • Smaller Donations: You can make a one-time donation via credit card through our online store. This quick and secure option allows you to support Firebird easily, and even small donations are recognized with special gift to show our gratitude.
  • Larger Donations: For more significant contributions, you can send your donation directly to one of our bank accounts. These donations play a crucial role in funding critical development projects and infrastructure improvements, and in return, you’ll receive exclusive gift to mark your generous support.

Your financial support, whether through individual contributions or organizational sponsorship, plays a vital role in shaping the future of Firebird. Join us today and become part of a global effort to keep Firebird thriving as a powerful, open-source solution for all.

Management

The supreme body of the Association is the General Meeting (a collective body consisting of all Members).

The statutory body of the Association is the Presidium - a three-member collective body elected by the General Meeting.
