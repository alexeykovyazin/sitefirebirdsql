December 12, 2007

Firebird 1.5.5 is Released

The Firebird Project team is pleased to announce the release of Firebird 1.5.5. This bug-fix release adds no new features but addresses a few bugs and security issues that have turned up in the 11 months since v.1.5.4.

November 22, 2007

Firebird Russian Conference

The second Russian Firebird and Interbase conference starts tomorrow in Moscow. It's the largest developer event for our Russian-speaking community — last year 200 people attended. Our core developers Dmitry Yemanov, Alex Peshkov and Vlad Horsun will be speakers, along with many other familiar names.
