Video "Problems during the migration", Part 2 of 4

In this video, Alexey Kovyazin, President of Firebird Foundation, and Edson Gregorio, CEO of MQFS, discuss possible problems during the migration.

There will be 4 episodes in the season devoted to migration: 3 videos and webinar with live questions and answers (to be announced).

We plan to publish videos every day, so stay tuned!
