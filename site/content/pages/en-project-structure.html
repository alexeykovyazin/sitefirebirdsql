The global coordination and management is performed by the Firebird Administration Group. This group also acts as the primary public contact for any outsider willing to communicate with the project.

The project hosts its main resources on GitHub, all the released binaries as well as the corresponding source code are available from there. The mailing lists can be found on either SourceForge Firebird Groups or Yahoo! Groups.

The development is supported by the Firebird Foundation. Also, the Foundation takes care of the legal interests of the Firebird Project as a whole and is the owner of the "Firebird" trademark.
