This year the Firebird Conference was focused on Firebird 4 and will also include a series of workshops: extended presentations and practical exercises. We want to make it more educational, more practical, and less theoretical. 

The Firebird Conference 2019 is Over! We will publish presentations and videos here soon.

Below you can find the detailed schedule of the conference. There will be several additional sessions, which will appear in the schedule a bit later, but all major talks are confirmed.

Organisation, Date, Place

The Firebird 2019 Conference is organised by IBPhoenix and IBSurgeon, and it will take place from October 17th to October 19th, in Berlin, Germany, at the hotel Holiday Inn Berlin City East - Landsberger Alle.
You can book rooms directly there (email sabine.anke@hibce.de) or use any suitable booking service, or choose another hotel nearby.

 

Registration

The registration is open. Please book your tickets here.

Please note, that accommodation (hotel) is not included in the conference fee.
