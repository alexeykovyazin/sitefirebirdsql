Join the Firebird Team!

The Firebird Foundation is the main organization behind the development of Firebird – essentially, we fund the C++ development of Firebird, drivers, documentation, and all other important things.

Firebird is an important part of your profession and your business. By becoming a supporter, you not only help fund the Firebird Project, but you also receive many benefits that will help in your daily work and influence your career.
