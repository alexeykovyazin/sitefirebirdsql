Free book "Detailed features of Firebird 5" is published

Dive deep into the revolutionary features of Firebird 5.0 with this comprehensive guide written by database expert Denis Simonov and edited by Alexey Kovyazin.

This book offers an in-depth exploration of the significant advancements that make Firebird 5.0 a pivotal release in the world of relational databases.

Whether you're a seasoned database administrator, a curious developer, or an IT professional looking to stay ahead of the curve, this book provides the knowledge and insights you need to leverage the full potential of Firebird 5.0.

About the Author:
Denis Simonov is a veteran developer and DBA, renowned for his contributions to official Firebird documentation, including the Firebird SQL Language Reference and Firebird Developer Guide (see all Firebird documentation here). His expertise shines through in this detailed exploration of Firebird 5.0's capabilities.

About the editor
Alexey Kovyazin, President of Firebird Foundation and CEO of IBSurgeon, brings a wealth of experience from his work with industry giants like Borland, Embarcadero, and Microsoft. Co-author of "The InterBase World" (2003) and former editor-in-chief of "The Interbase and Firebird Magazine" (2005-2006), Alexey's insights provide a unique perspective on Firebird's evolution and its place in the database ecosystem.

Together, these authors offer an unparalleled look into the future of relational databases with Firebird 5.0. Don't miss this opportunity to stay at the forefront of database technology!
