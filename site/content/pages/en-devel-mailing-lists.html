Here you can find the mailing lists used by the project team to communicate about the various aspects of the server development. The other mailing lists you might consider useful can be found here.

Firebird-devel

Primary forum for developers working with the Firebird source code, writing documentation, or developing other software (e.g. drivers) and needing the under-the-hood information about the server. It is also the place to report bugs against the unstable (Alpha and Beta) versions. Questions about porting to non mainstream platforms are welcome as well.

Do not use this list for support questions regarding the officially released versions!

List is available online at GoogleGroups.
Subscribe via e-mail: firebird-devel+subscribe@googlegroups.com
Archive of old messages is available on mail-archive.

Firebird-architect

This was a forum for asking about and discussing the architecture of Firebird, including ideas for future development. It has now been rolled up into firebird-devel.

Subscribe at firebird-devel.
List archive of the old list is available at Firebird Groups Archive

Firebird-checkins

Notification channel for all the commits happening in the Firebird source code, including sub-projects (drivers, documentation, etc). Messages are generated automagically by the CVS and SVN hooks. Use this forum to track the ongoing activity of the project developers.

Don't post anything to this list! The appropriate discussions should be initiated in Firebird-Devel instead!

Subscribe via e-mail: firebird-checkins-request@lists.sourceforge.net
List archive is available at SourceForge.net

Firebird-test

Forum for QA technicians.

Subscribe via e-mail: firebird-test-request@lists.sourceforge.net
List archive is available at SourceForge.net

Firebird-docs

This was a forum for documentation writers and translators. It has now been rolled up into firebird-devel.

Subscribe at firebird-devel.
List archive is available at SourceForge.net
