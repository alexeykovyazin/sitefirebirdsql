May 02, 2024

Firebird Camp in Brazil

On May 11, 2024, the event "Firebird Camp" will be held in Frederico Westphallen, RS, Brazil. This practical seminar is dedicated to enhancing the performance of Firebird databases. The event is organized by MQFS and will feature presentations by Edson Gregorio, CEO of MQFS, and Alexey Kovyazin, CEO of IBSurgeon and President of the Firebird Foundation. The seminar will be conducted in Portuguese.
