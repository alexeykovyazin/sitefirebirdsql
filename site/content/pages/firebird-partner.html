Firebird Partner monthly or annual donation

As a Firebird Partner, you become part of an exclusive community that not only contributes to the future of Firebird, but also enjoys a number of unique benefits. ​​ Here’s what you can look forward to:

  • Early access to EmberWings Magazine – You get a year's advance access to each quarterly issue, which is packed with articles, expert insights, interviews, tips and updates on Firebird's progress.​​
  • Special offers from our partners – You can take advantage of exclusive discounts and offers from our network of trusted partners.​​
  • Certification exams – You can take advantage of favorable conditions for signing up for a certified exam and become a certified professional.
  • Webinars – You may attend regular closed live webinars (via Zoom) covering important topics including performance, replication, security, and more.
  • Firebrid Conferences – You can take advantage of discounts on entrance fees for selected events.
  • Exclusive content – Access to technical content reserved just for subscribers.
  • Right to participate in Firebird release planning – Have your voice heard by taking part in discussions and planning for future Firebird releases.
  • Special gifts – At least twice a year, we issue special gifts (in the form of mystery boxes) as an expression of our gratitude for your support.

Please note that the details of individual benefits may change over time. However, this subscription will always have the highest level of benefits.
